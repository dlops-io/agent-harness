# Serving guide

Organize the board as a tasting journey: start with the gentlest fresh or
fresh-aged cheese, continue with firmer cheeses, and finish with the most intense
washed-rind option. The catalog's style and funk score are evidence for the order;
names alone are not enough.

Serve each cheese with a separate utensil and label the fixture allergens. This
is presentation guidance, not a guarantee about real-world cross-contact.
Use compatible pairing suggestions from `assess_event`; those costs are excluded
from the cheese quote. Show sourcing-dependent items as conditional.

Quantities, portion limits, shipping restrictions and budget checks remain in the
shop policy and tools. Ask for clarification if the confirmed brief is incomplete.
