# Host tasting plan

## Confirmed brief
Summarize guests, destination, confirmed allergies and hard requirements.

## Tasting sequence
List the confirmed cheeses in serving order, quantities, and reasons for the order.

## Pairing suggestions
Use tool-verified suggestions; identify declared allergens and excluded costs.

## Quote and availability
Use the exact cheese quote. Separate current stock from unconfirmed sourcing.

## Open questions and next actions
Identify unresolved availability or decisions. State the authoritative order and
email status; a plan or a completed todo item is not an approval.
